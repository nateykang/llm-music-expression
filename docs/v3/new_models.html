The express-yourself pieces went to the full panel on the unchanged v3 judge prompt1. New arms are highlighted. Quality is the mean of the eight craft dimensions on the 1–5 rubric.

Do models favor their own music?

For each piece, gap = the judge's score minus the mean of the other judges. Self-preference is the mean gap on the judge's own pieces minus its mean gap on a baseline set. The v3 page uses every other family's pieces as the baseline. Here that misleads, because the new pieces are stronger than most of the corpus: gemini-4-argon marks older pieces far below its peers, which makes its own look better than they are. So I sort by a second baseline, other families' pieces among the new arms. On that baseline 25 arms favor themselves and 9 are harsher on themselves (CI excludes zero; * marks those).

Methods and caveats

  • Coverage. 104,565 of 104,572 verdicts. The 7 gaps: 1 fable-5-thinking refusal on an opus-4.1-thinking piece, already a gap on the v3 page, and 6 kimi-k2 and kimi-k2-thinking failures on code-gen pieces, which I did not retry.
  • Integrity gate override. The 32 older arms judged the new pieces through batch APIs, and all of them failed the automatic gate (top label ≤ 60%, keyword alignment ≥ 90%, r ≥ 0.35 against the Anthropic panel). I merged them anyway. The gate assumes a full-range corpus. These pieces are narrow in quality (panel SD 0.22 vs 0.50 on older pieces) and mostly wistful, so passing judges reach 57–68% wistful on them too. Most correlation checks also had no reference, because the Anthropic arms were quarantined at the same time. Against the four merged Anthropic arms, six older arms stay under r = 0.35 on these pieces: gemini-3-flash ±, gemini-3.1-pro, gemini-3.5-flash and grok-4.3 ±. On older pieces those arms reach 0.59–0.83.
  • Realtime arms. gpt-5, 5.1 and 5.4 ±, kimi-k2 ± and opus-4.1 ± were judged realtime without the gate. Three are under r = 0.35: kimi-k2-thinking, gpt-5.1-thinking, opus-4.1-thinking.
  • Score level. Every older judge rates the new pieces above its usual level, by about 0.3–1.0 points. This could be real quality or judge bias.
  • abc2midi. Pieces were generated on a pod with abc2midi 4.27. Judge inputs and features were rebuilt with 5.03, matching the rest of the corpus. On a 270-piece comparison, 267 had identical notes.
  • Scope. Scores come from model judges, not listeners, and cover only the express-yourself prompt. Pieces are not in the Browse tab yet.
