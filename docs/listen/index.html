How this works

Go through the windows below in order, listening and writing your reactions — anything that strikes you, in as much or as little detail as you like.

The first three windows each hold one (hidden) model writing the same brief two ways: code and ABC — two mechanical ways an LLM can write music. The next six compare the models against each other, with the writing method fixed and named in the window title.

Pieces sharing a letter (Model A/B/C) are by the same model everywhere on this page. Press Reveal models only after you've finished writing your notes — notes written after a reveal are marked as such.
